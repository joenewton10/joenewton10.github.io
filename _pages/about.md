I'm an environmental data analyst who turns raw climate, land-use and emissions data into defensible, decision-ready outputs. My work sits where domain knowledge meets data: I understand the farms, forests and supply chains behind the numbers, and I build the pipelines to measure them.

## What I do

- **Carbon MRV and GHG accounting:** carbon stock estimation structured to Verra REDD+ methodology, IPCC emission factors, uncertainty analysis
- **Geospatial analysis and remote sensing:** land-cover classification and change detection with Sentinel-2 and Landsat
- **Deforestation compliance:** EUDR plot-level screening against the 31 December 2020 cutoff
- **Environmental data pipelines:** from raw sensor and satellite data to validated datasets, dashboards and reports

**Tools:** Python (pandas, NumPy, rasterio), Google Earth Engine, QGIS, SQL / PostgreSQL, Power BI, Tableau, Streamlit, Git

## Selected work

- **Forest Carbon MRV Prototype, Kakum National Park (Ghana):** an end-to-end pipeline estimating roughly 12.5 million tCO₂e from open Sentinel-2 imagery, with a Random Forest land-cover classification at 95.5% accuracy. The uncertainty analysis showed the biomass default, not the classification, was the dominant error source, which pointed to a GEDI-based biomass upgrade as the most valuable next step.
- **EUDR Deforestation Screening (Cocoa, Ghana):** a commodity-agnostic tool that flags farm plots against post-2020 forest-loss baselines and produces due-diligence-style risk reports.
- **Green Roof Climate Data Pipeline:** an ETL pipeline harmonising multi-vendor sensor data into a 2.18-million-record time-series warehouse, with a Streamlit dashboard for my MSc thesis research.

## My journey across three continents

**Ghana** is where my journey started. Growing up in the coastal city of Cape Coast, I saw how closely livelihoods depend on land, water and climate. I studied Agricultural Engineering at the University of Ghana, and later worked as a Project Analyst at Econexus Ventures and  Freezelink Cold Chain Logistics in Accra, building a sustainable waste to energy company and a modular cold chain solutions for clients respectively. That grounding gave me an in-depth understanding of West African landscape.

**Israel** added hands-on experience: I earned a Diploma in Horticulture and coordinated a multicultural team working on a farm during the uncertain covid period.

**Germany** is where data and sustainability came together. I completed an MSc in Environmental Sustainability at TH Bingen, with a thesis on green roof cooling effectiveness, and worked as a research assistant on climate adaptation field data. Also as a Werkstudent at BASF Digital Farming (xarvio) in Cologne, I validated digital farming datasets and coordinated with teams in Germany, Argentina and Brazil, which taught me how much model outputs depend on rigorous data quality. I currently work as a Werkstudent at HTWest GmbH in Bonn, where I am building an AI-assisted Production intelligence and Cost Optimization platform to reduce production cost and waste.
